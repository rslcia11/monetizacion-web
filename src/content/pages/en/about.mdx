Kriterio compares developer tools the way a senior colleague would explain them: a clear pick, the reasons behind it, and the trade-offs nobody puts on the pricing page.

## Why Kriterio exists

Many of the pages that answer "X vs Y" or "best X alternatives" are published by companies that sell one of the tools on the list. Others summarize product pages without ever using the tools. We wanted comparisons with a clear verdict, written by someone who builds with these tools, and with every fact linked to its official source.

## What you can expect

- A verdict at the top: which tool we would pick, and for whom.
- Advice from real projects: what to set up first and what to avoid.
- Code samples you can copy and run, based on each tool's official docs.
- Prices, limits and licenses checked on each vendor's own pages, with links.
- A visible update date, and fixes when a tool changes.

The full method is on <PageLink slug="how-we-test">How we test and research</PageLink>.

## How Kriterio is funded

Kriterio shows ads served by Google AdSense. No tool maker pays to be included, reviewed or ranked, and we don't sell any of the tools we cover. Ads don't change what we write or which tool wins.
