This policy explains what data is collected when you visit Kriterio ("we", "the site"), why, and what you can do about it.

## Who is responsible

Kriterio is run by {site.owner}, based in {site.city}, {site.country}, who is responsible for the personal data described here. Contact: <ContactEmail />.

## What we don't collect

Kriterio has no user accounts, no comments and no newsletter. We don't ask for your name or email to read anything. We don't use analytics tools at this time. Our fonts are served from our own domain, so reading an article doesn't send requests to font providers.

## Data collected when you visit

**Server logs.** Like any website, the servers that deliver Kriterio receive technical data with each request: your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time. Our hosting provider uses it to deliver the site and to protect it against abuse.

**Emails you send us.** If you write to us, we keep your message and email address to answer you, and delete them when they're no longer needed.

## Advertising and cookies

Kriterio shows ads served by Google AdSense.

- Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
- Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
- You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting [Google's Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/). You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).

To learn more, see [how Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

Where the law requires it, for example for visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a consent message asks for your choice before personalized ads are used. You can change your choice at any time from that message.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to or restrict its use, and to receive a copy of it. These rights are recognized by Ecuador's Organic Law on Personal Data Protection (LOPDP), the EU and UK GDPR, and US state laws such as California's CCPA.

To exercise them, write to <ContactEmail />. We don't hold any account or profile about you, so most requests will be about emails you sent us. For data collected through advertising cookies, use the Google options listed above.

If you think we haven't handled your data properly, you can complain to Ecuador's Superintendency for Personal Data Protection or to the data protection authority where you live.
