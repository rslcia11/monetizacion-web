Every comparison on Kriterio follows the rules on this page. Each article also ends with its own "How we tested" section, listing the exact versions, the test date and the machine we used.

## We use the tools ourselves

We install every tool we write about and use it for the tasks the article covers. We don't write comparisons from product pages or press releases.

## Same conditions for every tool

Within an article, every tool runs on the same machine, with the same data (for example, the same API collection) and the same steps. Otherwise the numbers wouldn't be comparable.

## Everything is recorded

For each test we record the tool version, the date and the environment: hardware and operating system. You'll find them at the end of every article, so you can tell whether a result still applies to the version you use.

## Only numbers we measured

Every number in our result tables comes from our own runs. When a measurement is still pending, the table shows a dash (—). We never fill a gap with an estimate or with a figure published by the vendor.

## Our own screenshots

Screenshots are taken by us during testing. We don't use vendors' marketing images.

## Facts we don't measure

Prices, licenses and supported platforms come from the vendor's official pages, and we link to them in the "Sources" section of each article. Prices and plans change often, so check the vendor's page before you buy.

## Independence

No tool maker pays to be included, reviewed or ranked, and we don't sell any of the tools we cover. The site is funded by ads served by Google AdSense, and advertisers have no say in what we write.

## Updates and corrections

Tools change fast. We re-check articles periodically and show the last update date at the top of each one. If something is wrong or out of date, write to <ContactEmail />. We'll fix it and update the date.
