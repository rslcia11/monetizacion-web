## What "good enough" means here

AppFlowy is built to feel like Notion: block-based pages, slash commands, and databases you view as a grid, board or calendar. So the question isn't whether it looks the same. It's whether it covers your daily work. That depends on three things: who edits with you, whether you need your data local, and which integrations you rely on.

One outdated argument first: Notion does work offline now. Since August 2025 you can mark pages as available offline in its desktop and mobile apps. AppFlowy's advantage is different. In local mode it never needs a server or an account at all.

## AppFlowy is good enough for one or two people

<ToolFacts tool="AppFlowy" price="Free; Pro $10/user/mo, yearly" runsOn="Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, Android, web" license="AGPL-3.0 (app)" />

Download the desktop app, pick local mode and everything stays in a folder on your machine. No sign-up. You need an account to sync between devices or invite someone.

The cloud Free plan covers one shared workspace with 2 members and 5 GB of storage, with unlimited pages and blocks. For a pair working together, that's more room than Notion's Free plan. Pro is $10 per member per month yearly and allows up to 50 members.

AppFlowy's AI features can run locally through Ollama, for free: chat, summarize, fix grammar, continue writing. Install Ollama, pull a model, and connect it in AppFlowy's settings:

```sh
ollama pull llama3.1
```

Moving from Notion works best on the cloud version. AppFlowy imports Notion's full ZIP export into a new cloud workspace. In local mode you can bulk-import the Markdown and CSV files from that export instead.

Self-hosting is free for one seat. Team self-hosting is $10 per seat per month, billed yearly, using AppFlowy's commercial server.

## Notion is still ahead for teams

<ToolFacts tool="Notion" price="Free; Plus $10/member/mo, yearly" runsOn="Web, Windows, macOS, iOS, Android" license="Proprietary" />

Notion's lead is everything around the editor: granular sharing and permissions, guests, a large template gallery, and integrations with the tools teams already use. Many people can edit the same page at once. For a company wiki or shared project docs, that maturity matters more than where the data sits.

The Free plan is great alone, but tight for teams. Once two or more people share a Free workspace, it gets a lifetime limit of 1,000 blocks. Plus costs $10 per member per month yearly, or $12 monthly.

## How to choose in 30 seconds

- **Just you, data stays local:** AppFlowy in local mode.
- **Two people, free:** AppFlowy's cloud Free plan.
- **A real team with sharing and integrations:** Notion.
- **You want AI without sending notes to the cloud:** AppFlowy with Ollama.

## FAQ

### Is AppFlowy really free?

The desktop app is free and open source under AGPL-3.0, and local mode needs no account. The cloud Free plan covers 2 members and 5 GB. Bigger teams pay $10 per member per month, billed yearly.

### Can I import my Notion workspace into AppFlowy?

Yes. On AppFlowy Cloud you can import Notion's full ZIP export into a new workspace. In local mode, bulk-import the Markdown and CSV files from that export.

### Is AppFlowy cheaper than Notion?

On paid plans they cost the same: $10 per member per month, billed yearly. AppFlowy is cheaper for a pair, because its Free plan covers two members without Notion's 1,000-block limit.
