Our picks
Why teams are leaving Notion
Notion is the standard for team wikis, but it lacks an offline mode. All your data lives on their servers. If you lose your internet connection, you cannot access your workspace.

Many users also worry about vendor lock-in and privacy. Because Notion is proprietary, you cannot audit its code or host it yourself. This pushes individuals and teams toward local-first alternatives where they can actually own their data files and read the underlying source code.

AppFlowy: the open-source Notion clone
AppFlowy is designed to look and work like Notion. It uses the same block-based editing system, slash commands, and database views. The free cloud plan includes 5 GB of storage, unlimited pages, and collaboration for up to two members.   

The core software is open source under the AGPL v3.0 license. You can self-host the community edition for free. If you want to self-host for a larger team, AppFlowy requires a commercial self-hosted license. The paid Pro cloud plan costs $10 per user per month when billed annually, raising the limits to 50 members and unlimited storage.

Anytype: built for extreme privacy
Anytype takes a different approach to data. It is a local-first application that uses peer-to-peer technology to sync your notes between your devices. Your knowledge base lives on your hard drive, not in a central cloud.   

The free plan gives you 100 MB of remote backup storage and limits you to 10 shared channels. If you need more backup space, the Plus plan costs $4 per month and upgrades your remote storage to 1 GB. Anytype describes its code as "source-available" rather than strictly open-source, meaning anyone can read the code but certain commercial uses are restricted.

You want a peer-to-peer sync network: Anytype.

FAQ
Are these tools actually open source?
AppFlowy is open source under the AGPL v3.0 license, though it uses a separate commercial license for enterprise self-hosting. Anytype is classified as "source-available"; you can read and modify the code, but the license restricts certain commercial applications.   

Can I use AppFlowy offline?
Yes. Unlike Notion, AppFlowy supports working locally on your device. You can even configure its AI features, called Vault Workspace, to run entirely on your local hardware without transferring data over the internet.   

Does Anytype charge per user?
No. Anytype's paid plans are based on the amount of remote backup storage you consume, starting at $4 per month for 1 GB. It does not use the standard per-seat billing model for individual accounts.
