The verdict
The offline and open-source gap
Notion changed how teams organize information, but its architecture forces you into a specific model. Your data lives on Notion's servers, and you cannot access your workspace if you lose your internet connection. You also cannot inspect the code or host the application on your own hardware.

AppFlowy was built to solve these exact problems. It provides a familiar block-based editor and database views, but its core is open source. It defaults to storing data locally, allowing you to work completely offline, and it gives you the option to self-host your workspace to avoid vendor lock-in.

AppFlowy: local-first and open source
AppFlowy is built with Flutter and Rust to provide native desktop and mobile applications. Because it saves data locally, it runs faster than web-based alternatives and works without an internet connection.   

The free cloud plan includes 5 GB of storage and allows you to collaborate with one other member. To add more members or get unlimited storage, the Pro plan costs $10 per user per month when billed annually. If you prefer not to use their cloud, you can self-host the community edition on your own infrastructure.

Notion: the cloud standard
Notion is a fully managed cloud workspace. It excels at real-time collaboration, allowing dozens of people to edit the same page simultaneously. The free plan is excellent for single users, offering unlimited pages and blocks.   

However, the free plan restricts team workspaces to a maximum of 1,000 blocks. To remove this limit, you must upgrade to the Plus plan at $10 per user per month. Since Notion is a proprietary, cloud-only service, you cannot self-host it, and an internet connection is always required to read or edit your notes.

How to choose in 30 seconds
You want to work completely offline: AppFlowy.

You want to self-host your company wiki: AppFlowy.

You need real-time collaboration for a large team: Notion.

You want a free personal workspace with unlimited blocks: Notion.

FAQ
Can I use AppFlowy offline?
Yes. AppFlowy is a local-first application. It saves your data directly to your device, meaning you can read, write, and organize your notes without an active internet connection.   

Is AppFlowy cheaper than Notion?
For teams, the pricing is identical. Both AppFlowy and Notion charge $10 per user per month on their standard paid plans when billed annually. However, AppFlowy allows you to self-host the open-source version to avoid per-user cloud fees entirely.   

Can I host Notion on my own server?
No. Notion is a proprietary, closed-source product. All data is stored and managed on their central servers, and they do not offer a self-hosted version for any pricing tier.
