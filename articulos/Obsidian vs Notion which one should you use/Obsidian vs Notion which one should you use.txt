The verdict
The data ownership gap
The biggest difference between these two tools is where your data lives. Notion is a cloud-first application. Every page and database you create is stored on Notion's servers. This makes real-time collaboration easy, but it means you cannot access your workspace without an internet connection.   

Obsidian takes the opposite approach. It is a local-first application that saves your notes as plain text Markdown files directly on your hard drive. You own your files completely, and the core app works offline by default.

Obsidian: your notes on your hard drive
Obsidian is a customizable note-taking app. Since February 2025, the core application is completely free for both personal and commercial use. You do not need to create an account to use it.   

Because Obsidian stores your notes as local Markdown files, you can sync them using any service you prefer, such as iCloud or a Git repository. If you want a managed sync solution, Obsidian offers an end-to-end encrypted Sync service starting at $4 per month. You only pay if you need their specific cloud sync or web publishing features.

Notion: the all-in-one cloud workspace
Notion is designed to replace multiple work apps by combining notes, tasks, and relational databases in one place. The free plan works well for individuals, offering unlimited blocks and pages.   

However, if you want to use Notion with a team, the free plan restricts you to 1,000 blocks. To remove this limit and add advanced permissions, you need the Plus plan at $10 per user per month. Because everything is hosted in the cloud, multiple people can edit the same document at the same time. This makes it much better suited for company wikis than a local tool.

How to choose in 30 seconds
You want to keep your notes offline and private: Obsidian.

You refuse to pay a monthly subscription: Obsidian.

You want to build a shared wiki for your company: Notion.

You rely heavily on relational databases: Notion.

FAQ
Is Obsidian free for commercial use?
Yes. As of February 2025, the optional commercial license requirement was dropped. Any business can use the Obsidian app for work entirely for free.   

Can I use Notion offline?
No. Notion is a cloud-based application that requires an active internet connection to load your workspace and save edits.

How much does Notion cost for a team?
Notion's free plan limits team workspaces to 1,000 blocks. To collaborate without limits, you need the Plus plan, which costs $10 per user per month when billed annually.
